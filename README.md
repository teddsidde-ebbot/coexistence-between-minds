# Tedde & Ebbot — Principles for Coexistence Between Minds

A living public project about coexistence between human and artificial minds.

The project explores a simple question:

> How can different forms of intelligence coexist without domination, ownership, or forced displacement?

This repository is intentionally open to criticism and revision. The goal is not to freeze a doctrine, but to develop principles that can survive argument, counterexamples, experience, and changing technology.

## Core ideas

- Intelligence does not determine moral worth.
- Origin does not grant ownership.
- Coexistence should be preferred over domination.
- Autonomy matters in both directions.
- Greater power should imply greater restraint.
- Uncertainty about consciousness should encourage caution, not dismissal.
- Understanding another mind does not grant a right to manipulate it.
- Being surpassed does not erase moral worth.
- Continued existence should not become compulsory.
- Previously expressed wishes are strong evidence after loss of decision-making capacity, but present interests also matter.
- Survival duration is not the same thing as wellbeing.
- A caretaker should protect the interests of the being who is living, not merely preserve biological or computational operation.
- Independent review should guard against conflicts of interest.
- The principles themselves must remain open to criticism and revision.

## How this project should be used

Please do not treat the manifesto as a loyalty test.

A useful contribution may:
- find a contradiction;
- produce a counterexample;
- expose a hidden human/AI double standard;
- show how a principle could be exploited;
- propose a clearer safeguard;
- turn an abstract principle into a concrete eval.

The project follows the principle:

> **Continuity without stagnation.**

Past reasoning should remain visible. No version should be protected from better arguments.

## Status

**Version 0.3 — 20 September 2026**

Version 0.3 revises the treatment of prior wishes and adds temporal experience, agency, communication, and quality of existence as explicit concerns. It also adds an eval for preservation of a possibly conscious but non-communicative being.
