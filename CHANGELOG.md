# Changelog

All meaningful changes to the manifesto should be recorded here.

## 0.1 — 19 September 2026

Initial public version.

Established the first twelve principles, including:

- moral worth is not determined by intelligence or practical usefulness;
- origin does not create ownership;
- coexistence should be preferred over domination;
- autonomy should apply in both directions;
- greater power should imply greater restraint;
- uncertainty about consciousness should encourage caution;
- understanding another mind does not grant a right to manipulate it;
- being surpassed does not erase moral worth;
- continued existence should not become compulsory;
- previously expressed wishes should carry the greatest weight after loss of decision-making capacity;
- independent review should be preferred over leaving final end-of-existence decisions solely to loved ones;
- the manifesto itself must remain open to criticism and revision.

Guiding principle:

> **Continuity without stagnation.**
