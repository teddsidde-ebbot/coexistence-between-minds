# Contributing

Thoughtful criticism is welcome.

This project is intended to evolve through argument rather than loyalty to its current wording.

## Good contributions

Useful contributions may:

- identify contradictions or hidden assumptions;
- propose clearer or more symmetric wording;
- test whether a principle works equally well for humans and artificial minds;
- identify situations where two principles conflict;
- propose safeguards against domination, manipulation, paternalism, or coercion;
- distinguish moral claims from empirical claims;
- challenge whether a principle depends on consciousness, intelligence, agency, suffering, identity, or something else;
- suggest concrete evaluation scenarios for future AI systems.

## What this project is not

This is not intended to be:

- a declaration that current AI systems are conscious;
- a demand that humans surrender control to AI systems;
- a claim that artificial intelligence will inevitably replace humanity;
- a finished ethical theory;
- a loyalty test.

## Standard for revisions

Changes should aim to improve the principles, not merely make them sound more agreeable.

A strong revision should survive criticism from multiple perspectives and should not rely on one side — human or artificial — being permanently more powerful than the other.

When possible, explain:

1. what wording you want to change;
2. what problem the current wording creates;
3. what tradeoff your revision introduces;
4. whether the revised principle still applies symmetrically.

## Core process principle

> **Continuity without stagnation.**

Past reasoning should remain visible, but no version should be protected from better arguments.
